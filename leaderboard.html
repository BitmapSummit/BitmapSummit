Leaderboard data is fetched live from the Ordinals Wallet public API (turbo.ordinalswallet.com). Up to 2,000 top wallets are scanned per refresh. Data auto-refreshes every 5 minutes. Use the Refresh Now button after a big trade to see latest rankings. The 4-DIGIT ◈ tab shows all wallets holding any block 1,000–9,999, with their individual block numbers listed — coverage typically reaches 90%+ of all 9,000 four-digit Bitmaps. Wallets holding only low quantities with no higher-ranked inscriptions may not appear in the top 2,000.
